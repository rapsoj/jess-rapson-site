# UN Presentation

Squeezed in some time to work this month as well outside of the trip. The INFLOW-AI project is wrapping up nicely, with me finishing a draft of our paper before the end of the month. Also got to attend a UNHCR presentation where my model was referenced (no need for me to even present, the entire process is now automated).

![no-alignment]({{ '/assets/images/updates/2025-april/un-presentation.png' | absolute_url }})
*Pleased to see all my work having a direct impact on decision-making.*

I am, however, truly starting to understand the meaning of [hidden technical debt](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2015/file/86df7dcfd896fcaf2674f757a2463eba-Paper.pdf) in machine learning models. I am constantly messaged to fix the model, comment on model results, and fix faulty input data sources whose methods have changed since I built the model. It seems there is no clear easy way to just deploy a live model out in the word without maintenance costs. An interesting problem to consider for the future.
