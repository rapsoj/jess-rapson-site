# Halloween and Other Activities

Not much to report for this month. Continuing progress on the INFLOW-AI model. Details are technical and not overly-visualisable.

Alec and I celebrated Halloween this year with a house warming and costume party. Inspired by the YouTube Channel *Great Art Explained* (specifically his video on [Madame X and Dr. Pozzi, which I highly recommend](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cuHgxPe3J7I), Alec and I dressed as the eponymous muses. We visited both paintings at the Tate Britain earlier in the year, and really liked the artist and historical controversy surrounding both paintings (Madame X was a French socialite originally painted with a scandalous shoulder strap sliding off her shoulder, Dr. Pozzi was a womanising gynaecologist dressed almost like a Catholic cardinal in the portrait).
