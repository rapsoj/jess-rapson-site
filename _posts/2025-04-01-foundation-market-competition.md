I wrote a policy paper for the G7 arguing for increased scrutiny and regulation of anti-competitive practices made by dominant players in the foundation model market.

https://www.think7.org/publications/a-g7-strategy-for-ai-competition-and-consumer-rights/

# Project Overview

| **Motivation** | Foundation model markets are at a critical point where they risk being entirely dominated by a small handful of dominant firms, risking supply chains and increasing global inequality as foundation models are increasingly incorporated into downstream applications |
| **Output** | Policy brief explaining concerns with anti-competitive practices in the foundation model market and suggesting concrete actions for G7 Competition Authorities to take that can remedy the situation |
| **Client** | The G7 |
| **Status** | Complete |
| **Outcome** | Policy recommendations made at Think7 summit and included in Think7 Communiqué |

Full report [here](https://www.think7.org/publications/a-g7-strategy-for-ai-competition-and-consumer-rights/).
